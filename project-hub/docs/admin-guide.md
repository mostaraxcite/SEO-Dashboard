# Admin guide

**Settings** (sidebar, admins only):

- **General** — workspace name, mode (*Simple work* or *Requests + work*), time zone (drives SLA and digests), default language, SLA on/off, "only managers can deliver".
- **Branding** — brand color (automatically darkened/lightened until labels meet 4.5:1 contrast; status colors never change), portal display name, welcome text (EN/AR), logo (PNG/JPG/WebP ≤ 1 MB; only upload logos you're authorized to use).
- **Team** — invite staff (admin, manager, member, guest), change roles. Invitations are single-use, expire in 7 days, and fix the role/client server-side.
- **Request types** — labels/help in both languages, show/hide, order, default owner for routing. Follow-up questions come from the preset (Marketing, Agency, General).
- **Service levels** — first-response and triage targets in business hours, warning threshold, pause-on-needs-info, work days/hours, holidays.
- **Notifications** — in-app and email on/off; SMS (sender ID, monthly segment quota, per-recipient cooldown, allowed events). SMS still needs each recipient's opt-in + consented phone.
- **AI** — master switch, per-feature switches, requester access to *Improve with AI*, monthly action limit, spend cap, per-person hourly limit, usage.
- **Usage & logs** — notification delivery log (including *Not sent* reasons) and the append-only audit log.

Clients/departments are created under **Clients** (agency) or **Departments** (internal); invite their contacts from the client page.
