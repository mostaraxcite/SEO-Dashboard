# Team guide

- **My work** — what's assigned to you: *Needs my attention* (overdue, blocked, in review), *Due soon*, *Later*, plus dependencies you're accountable for. Add a task with the quick-add row.
- **Inbox** (managers) — new requests. Open one and use the **Triage** panel: *Accept* (owner + committed date or "flexible"), *Need info* (the requester sees your question), or *Decline* (public reason + optional internal reason).
- **Request page** — *Overview* (brief, follow-up answers, files, versions, timeline), *Conversation* (visible to the requester), *Internal work* (tasks, dependencies, internal notes — never visible to clients).
- **AI** — *Summarize*, *What's next?*, *Suggest tasks*. Results are drafts: edit, then *Review & create* / *Save as internal note*, or *Dismiss*. AI never proposes dates or people.
- **Deliver** — the only action that tells the requester work is ready. Requires a file, link or note; creates a new version; optionally asks for approval. Marking tasks *Done* never notifies the client.
- **Dates** — the requested date (from the requester) and the committed date (yours) are separate. Changing a commitment needs a reason and notifies the requester.
